How it works

Three steps from upload to an explained verdict

The same pipeline the research notebooks use, served through a FastAPI inference endpoint.

1

Face detection and crop

MTCNN locates the largest face, crops it with a 20 px margin and resizes it to 224 × 224, exactly as in training. Backgrounds and file metadata never reach the classifier.

2

Classification

A ViT-B/16 backbone splits the crop into 196 patches and runs 12 self-attention layers. A frequency branch over the FFT spectrum catches periodic generator artifacts the spatial branch can miss.

3

Attention rollout

Attention weights are multiplied through every layer to trace the decision back to image patches, producing a heatmap you can check against the face rather than a bare score.

Why this detector

Designed to be inspected, not just trusted

Architecture-native explanations

Heatmaps come from the transformer's own attention weights, not a post-hoc approximation bolted onto a CNN.

Frequency-aware

A lightweight convolutional branch over the log-magnitude FFT spectrum is fused with the ViT embedding before classification.

Cross-dataset tested

Train-on-one, test-on-the-other ablations quantify how much accuracy survives an unseen manipulation method.

Custom-data fine-tuned

All five checkpoints were adapted to fully AI-generated portraits with replay training, reaching 80 to 88% on that new fake type.

Benchmarks

Three architectures, one protocol

Every model is trained and evaluated on the same identity-disjoint manifest drawn from FaceForensics++ (c23) and Celeb-DF-v2, so the comparison is like for like.
