Input requirements

Matches the API's validation and the training preprocessing.

  • Format: JPEG or PNG. Other types are rejected with HTTP 400.
  • Content: at least one visible face. The largest face is cropped with MTCNN; images without a face return HTTP 422.
  • Quality: frontal or near-frontal faces of roughly 224 px or larger work best.
  • Scope: trained on face-swap video frames and AI-generated portraits. Non-face manipulations are out of scope.
