DeepGuard

Verify a face image and see why the model decided.

A Vision Transformer classifier with attention-rollout explanations, trained on FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF-v2 and fine-tuned on a custom set of AI-generated portraits.

  • Real / fake verdict with a confidence score
  • Attention heatmap showing the regions that drove the decision
  • Automatic face detection and cropping with MTCNN

Thesis research prototype · 97.47% test accuracy on the combined FF++ / Celeb-DF-v2 split
