Frequently asked questions

Is my image stored?

In the demo build, no. Images stay in your browser. The production API processes the upload in memory and returns the result without persisting the file.

Why did the detector reject my image?

The API only accepts JPEG and PNG, and it requires at least one detectable face. Very small, heavily occluded, or profile faces can fail MTCNN detection.

Can I trust the confidence score?

Treat it as a signal, not proof. In-domain accuracy is above 97%, but performance drops on manipulation methods the model has not seen. Review the Research page for the cross-dataset results.

What does the heatmap mean?

It shows which image patches received the most attention on the path to the decision. Bright regions influenced the verdict more. It is a qualitative aid, not a pixel-level proof of manipulation.

Does it work on video?

The models were trained on video frames, but the service currently accepts single images. Extract frames and submit them individually.
